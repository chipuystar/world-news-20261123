# The news of Monday 23 November 2026 — stories chosen for this entry

Each story below happened, or was first reported, on Monday 23 November 2026
(in some time zone). Six stories were picked for consequence and breadth:
climate, politics, war, markets, disaster and sport, across five regions.

## 1. COP31 climate talks end without a deal — Antalya, Türkiye (climate / global)
After running some 48 hours into overtime — the longest UN climate summit on
record — COP31 ended without an overarching agreement: the EU and others
refused a final text that made no mention of fossil fuels, and conference
president Murat Kurum conceded there was no consensus. UN Secretary-General
António Guterres said he was "deeply disappointed."
Sources: Associated Press via Barchart, "Why the failure to reach a major deal
at UN climate talks matters" (23 Nov 2026); Phys.org, "COP31 fails to agree on
climate deal in chaotic finale"; World News Wire top-10, 23 Nov 2026.
Note: the closing plenary ran late into Sunday night in Antalya and the
collapse was the lead story of Monday 23 November worldwide.

## 2. Democrats clinch a historic US House majority (politics / United States)
Democrats secured at least 258 seats in the US House of Representatives —
the largest House majority for either party since 1992 — after a North
Carolina recount flipped a seat their way.
Sources: Associated Press via World News Wire (23 Nov 2026); Inshorts, 23 Nov
2026; One Turn politics roundup, Monday 23 Nov 2026.

## 3. Ukraine says naval drones sank a Russian warship off Libya (war / Europe–Africa)
President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Ukrainian naval drones severely damaged
the Russian Navy corvette Merkury off the coast of Libya and also hit the
Russian dry cargo vessel MV Angara — a long-range strike far from the Black Sea.
Sources: Inshorts world news, Monday 23 Nov 2026 (13:02); WH News, "Ukrainian
Forces Claim to Sink Russian Warship Off Libya Coast" (Monday).

## 4. Markets rebound after the AI sell-off rout (economy / global)
US stock futures rebounded sharply on Monday morning — Nasdaq 100 futures up
about 3% — after Friday's AI-led rout pushed the S&P 500 and Dow into
correction; Asian authorities stepped in to support their markets, with
China's state funds buying a record amount of ETFs and South Korea preparing
a stabilisation fund.
Sources: Reuters via WTBX, "Wall Street futures rebound after rough week for
AI-linked shares" (23 Nov 2026); Bloomberg reporting summarised in GGMA,
"Nasdaq Futures Rebound as Asia Props Up Stocks" (23 Nov 2026).

## 5. Deadly factory fire in Indonesia (disaster / Asia)
At least 24 people were killed in a fire at a candy factory in Indonesia's
Banten province, with about 30 people still unaccounted for as the blaze was
still not fully out.
Source: Xinhua via World News Wire top-10, 23 Nov 2026.

## 6. Alcaraz wins his first ATP Finals title (sport / Europe)
Carlos Alcaraz beat Alexander Zverev 6-2, 7-6 in Turin to win his first ATP
Finals title, closing the tennis season on top.
Sources: Apex Sports News (23 Nov 2026); All Sport Life archive, Nov 2026.
Note: the final was played on Sunday evening in Turin and reported
worldwide on Monday 23 November.
